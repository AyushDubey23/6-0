How to Play 6a0 Cricket Draft Game

Step 1: Roll a Tournament Squad

In each draft round, an authentic tournament squad (e.g. 2011 India, 1999 Australia, 2019 England, 1992 Pakistan) is randomly revealed for your scouting review.

Step 2: Draft 1 Player Per Round

Pick 1 player from the squad to fill a required position on your Playing XI pitch: Openers (2), Middle-Order Batsmen (3), Wicketkeeper (1), All-Rounders (1-2), or Pace/Spin Bowlers (3-4).

Step 3: Tactical Team & Year Rerolls

Need a legendary all-rounder or express fast bowler? Use your limited squad or edition rerolls strategically to hunt for tournament champions and optimize team balance.

Step 4: Simulate Matches & Climb the Leaderboard

Review your team balance radar, then take your roster into the World Cup tournament simulation or battle head-to-head with friends in live multiplayer rooms.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is 6a0 Cricket Draft & World Cup Simulator?

6a0 is a 100% free online strategic cricket drafting game and World Cup tournament simulator. Players scout authentic historical tournament squads, draft an 11-player Playing XI respecting position constraints, and simulate realistic ball-by-ball cricket matches.

How does the cricket draft system work in 6a0?

Each round reveals a historical national squad (e.g., 2011 India, 2019 England, 1999 Australia). You select 1 player per turn into your Playing XI (Openers, Middle Order, All-Rounder, Bowlers, Wicketkeeper). You can use tactical rerolls to change teams or tournament years.

Can I play 6a0 multiplayer with friends?

Yes! 6a0 features real-time 1v1 multiplayer draft rooms powered by Firebase. Create a 6-character room code, share the invite link with a friend, conduct a live snake draft with turn timers, and battle in an interactive coin toss and simulated head-to-head match.

Is 6a0 completely free to play?

Yes, 6a0 is 100% free with no paywalls, microtransactions, subscriptions, or real-money gambling. It is an open sports strategy simulation platform designed for cricket enthusiasts.

Which tournament editions and squads are included?

6a0 features international cricket squads from iconic ICC Men's Cricket World Cups and T20 World Cups, featuring legendary rosters from 1992, 1996, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2011, 2015, 2019, 2023, and recent T20 editions.

How does the ball-by-ball match simulation work?

The simulation engine models authentic cricket physics and probabilities including batsman strike rates, bowler economy rates, wicket probabilities, pitch conditions, run chases, wagon wheels, and net run rate (NRR) calculations.
